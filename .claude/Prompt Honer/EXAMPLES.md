# Ready-to-Paste Prompts

25 prompts you can drop directly into Stitch, v0, Lovable, or Bolt. Every prompt has unique colors, specific button treatments, custom SVG descriptions, and enough design detail to kill generic output dead.

No two prompts share a palette. No two share a button style. Copy. Paste. Build something nobody's seen before.

## SaaS & Software

### 1. AI Code Review Platform

```
A dark, information-dense dashboard landing page for an AI code review
tool called "Sentinel" that catches bugs before your team does. Built
for engineering leads at 20-100 person startups who review 50+ PRs a week.

Hero section: 100vh height. Background: a subtle animated grid of faint
code characters scrolling vertically at 0.5px/s behind a dark overlay.
Headline in two parts — "Your codebase has" (medium weight, cream) then
"blind spots." (massive, italic serif, signal red). Below: a single CTA
"Scan your first repo" as a raised button with a 2px bottom shadow in

Show "2,847 repos scanned this week" with a pulsing green dot.

Below hero: Three feature cards that look like actual terminal windows.
Each card has a dark title bar with three dots (red/yellow/green),

badge. Card 2 shows a live-typing effect of an AI comment being written.
Card 3 shows a dependency tree as an inline SVG — nodes connected by
curved paths, with one node pulsing red to indicate a vulnerability.

Custom SVG for this project: A stylized eye icon made from two
concentric circles with a code bracket </> as the pupil. Used in
the navbar and as a watermark at 3% opacity on section backgrounds.

Social proof: A horizontal scroll of company logos (monochrome, 40%
opacity, brightens to 70% on hover). Below: "Catches 3.2x more issues
than manual review" as a stat block with the number in massive
monospace red.

Style: Background #0B0B11, surface #14141F, text #E8E6E1, text muted
#6B6977, accent Signal Red #E63B2E, success #2DD4A0, border #1F1F2E.
Headings: Space Grotesk 700. Body: Inter 400 at 15px/1.6. Code: Fira
Code 400. Global noise overlay at 0.04 opacity using inline SVG
feTurbulence filter. All containers rounded-2xl (16px radius).
```

### 7. Sleep Tracking App (5 Screens)

```
A dark, biometric-feeling sleep tracking app called "Drift" for
stressed professionals aged 28-45 who know they sleep badly but
don't know why. The app connects to a wearable and shows sleep
stage data.

This should feel like looking at your own biology — intimate,
precise, slightly sci-fi. Not a wellness app. A diagnostic tool
that happens to look beautiful.

Create 5 screens:

1. Dashboard — Dark void background (#0A0A14). Top: greeting
"Good morning, Sarah" in light lavender, current sleep score "72"
in a large circular SVG gauge (arc from 0-100, filled in plasma
purple with a glow, unfilled in dark gray). Inside the circle:
score number in Sora 700, "of 100" small below. Below: last night's
sleep stages as a horizontal stacked bar SVG — Awake (red), REM
(plasma purple), Light (steel blue), Deep (indigo) — with time
labels. "7h 23m total" below. Three quick stat pills: "REM: 1h 48m",
"Deep: 2h 12m", "Interruptions: 2".

2. Sleep timeline — Full-width SVG hypnogram (the wavy chart
showing sleep stage transitions through the night). X-axis: time
(11pm to 7am). Y-axis: Awake/REM/Light/Deep. The line is a smooth
bezier path in plasma purple with the area below filled in gradient
(dark at bottom, purple at top, 30% opacity). Tap points on the
chart show event markers: "Heart rate spike" or "Movement detected"
with a small icon.

3. Trends — Weekly view with 7 vertical bars (one per night), each
segmented by sleep stage colors. Below: a sparkline SVG of sleep
score trend over 30 days. An insight card at bottom: "Your deep
sleep improved 23% since you started the wind-down routine" with
a small upward arrow icon.

4. Wind-down routine — A checklist-style screen. 4 items: "Blue
light filter" (phone icon), "Journal entry" (pen icon), "Breathing
exercise" (lungs icon), "Room temperature check" (thermometer icon).
Each item has a toggle switch. Completed items show a subtle checkmark
and dim to 60% opacity. A circular countdown SVG at the top shows
"45 min until target bedtime".

5. Settings — Dark surface cards for each setting group. Sleep goal
time picker (custom roller-style input), wearable connection status
with a pulsing Bluetooth SVG icon and device name, notification
preferences, and data export button.

Custom SVG set: Moon phases (5 phases from new to full) in a 1.5px
stroke style, used as section dividers and loading indicators. A
constellation-dot pattern used as a subtle background texture (random
dots at 3% opacity connected by faint lines).

Style: Background Deep Void #0A0A14, surface #151520, elevated
#1E1E2E, text Ghost #F0EFF4, text muted #6B6880, accent Plasma
Purple #7B61FF, secondary Steel Blue #4A8FBF, error Warm Red
#FF6B6B, success Mint #2DD4A0, border rgba(123,97,255,0.15).
Font: Sora 600 for headings, Inter 400 for body at 14px, Fira Code
for data. Bottom tab nav with custom SVG icons. Containers: 16px
radius.

Button style: Plasma purple background with a subtle inner glow
(inset box-shadow 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.1) on top, creating
a slight convex/glossy feel). On tap, the glow inverts to an inset
press. Feels like a backlit key.
```

## How to Use These Prompts

1. **Copy any prompt above and paste it directly into Stitch**
2. **Customize the specifics**: Change the brand name, colors, content, and SVG descriptions to match your project
3. **Iterate one thing at a time**: After the first result, use the follow-up templates in PROMPT-FORMULAS.md
4. **Run through the ANTI-SLOP checklist** before calling it done

Every prompt includes unique colors (no two share a palette), custom SVG elements specific to the brand, and button treatments with physical depth. The specificity is what kills generic output.
