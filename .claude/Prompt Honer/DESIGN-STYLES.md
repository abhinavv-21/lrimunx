# Design Styles Reference

30 named design styles. Each one includes what it looks like, when to use it, a starter color palette, and a prompt snippet you can drop directly into Stitch.

---

## 1. Stripe Minimal

**What it looks like:** Clean white backgrounds, precise spacing, subtle gradients on CTAs, restrained color use. Typography does the heavy lifting. Feels expensive without trying hard.

**Best for:** SaaS, fintech, developer tools, API products

**Palette:** Background `#ffffff`, text `#0a2540`, accent `#635bff`, secondary `#00d4aa`

**Prompt snippet:** `Clean, minimal SaaS landing page. White background, deep navy text, purple accent buttons. Inspired by Stripe's design language. Generous whitespace, sharp typography hierarchy.`

---

## 2. Linear Dark

**What it looks like:** Dark interface with high-contrast text, subtle gradients on dark surfaces, purple-blue accents. Information-dense but calm. Command palette aesthetic.

**Best for:** Productivity tools, project management, dev tools, dashboards

**Palette:** Background `#0a0a0a`, surface `#1a1a1a`, accent `#5e6ad2`, text `#e8e8e8`

**Prompt snippet:** `Dark-themed productivity app interface. Near-black background, subtle card surfaces, purple-blue accent. Dense but organized. Inspired by Linear's dark UI.`

---

## 3. Notion Editorial

**What it looks like:** Off-white backgrounds, serif headings mixed with sans-serif body, generous line height, block-based layout. Feels like a well-typeset document.

**Best for:** Content platforms, wikis, knowledge bases, blogs, portfolio sites

**Palette:** Background `#ffffff`, text `#37352f`, accent `#2eaadc`, muted `#9b9a97`

**Prompt snippet:** `Clean editorial layout with serif headings and sans-serif body text. Off-white background, warm brown-black text. Block-based content sections. Notion-inspired reading experience.`

---

## 4. Glassmorphism

**What it looks like:** Frosted glass cards with backdrop blur, subtle borders, layered depth. Translucent surfaces floating over colorful or blurred backgrounds.

**Best for:** Music apps, finance dashboards, creative tools, mobile apps

**Palette:** Background gradient, card `rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`, border `rgba(255,255,255,0.3)`, blur `20px`

**Prompt snippet:** `Glassmorphism UI with frosted translucent cards floating over a gradient background. Subtle white borders, backdrop blur on all surfaces. Layered depth effect.`

---

## 5. Neubrutalism

**What it looks like:** Thick black borders, solid drop shadows offset 4-8px, flat bright colors, no gradients. Looks like a physical sticker sheet. Intentionally raw.

**Best for:** Creative agencies, portfolio sites, startup landing pages, playful brands

**Palette:** Background `#e8e4de`, accent `#ff6b35`, shadow `#000000`, cards `#ffffff`

**Prompt snippet:** `Neubrutalist design with thick black borders, offset drop shadows, flat bright accent colors. No gradients. Chunky, intentionally imperfect layout. Think anti-corporate.`

---

## 6. Japandi

**What it looks like:** Merge of Japanese minimalism and Scandinavian warmth. Muted earth tones, natural textures, extreme whitespace. Serene and grounded.

**Best for:** Wellness brands, home goods, tea/ceramics shops, spa websites, meditation apps

**Palette:** Background `#f5f0eb`, text `#2d2926`, accent `#8b7355`, secondary `#c4b5a0`

**Prompt snippet:** `Japandi-styled product page. Warm cream background, muted earth tones, extreme whitespace. Soft serif headings. Photography-forward with natural textures. Calm and grounded.`

---

## 7. Y2K / Retro Web

**What it looks like:** Chunky pixel fonts, bright neon gradients, chrome/metallic effects, star/sparkle decorations. Nostalgic early-internet energy.

**Best for:** Fashion brands targeting Gen Z, creative portfolios, music artists, event pages

**Palette:** Background `#0d0d0d`, neon pink `#ff2d95`, neon blue `#00f0ff`, chrome `#c0c0c0`

**Prompt snippet:** `Y2K retro web aesthetic. Neon pink and cyan on dark background. Chrome metallic accents, pixel-style decorative elements. Bold, nostalgic, early-internet energy.`

---

## 8. Figma/Vercel Developer

**What it looks like:** Dark mode with monospace accents, code-block-style cards, terminal green or blue highlights. Feels like a polished dev tool.

**Best for:** Developer documentation, API landing pages, open-source projects, CLI tools

**Palette:** Background `#000000`, surface `#111111`, green `#00dc82`, code text `#e4e4e7`

**Prompt snippet:** `Developer-focused dark mode landing page. Monospace code blocks, terminal-green accents on pure black. Clean grid layout. Inspired by Vercel and Figma's developer docs.`

---

## 9. Aesop Editorial Luxury

**What it looks like:** Photography dominates. Serif typography with extreme tracking. Muted palette. Every element has breathing room. Feels like a print magazine.

**Best for:** Luxury retail, skincare, fragrance, wine, premium fashion

**Palette:** Background `#faf7f2`, text `#1a1714`, accent `#6b5b4b`, border `#d4c8b8`

**Prompt snippet:** `Luxury editorial product page. Cream background, dark warm text, wide serif headings with generous letter spacing. Photography-first layout. Feels like a high-end print catalog. Think Aesop or Le Labo.`

---

## 10. Gradient Mesh

**What it looks like:** Large flowing gradient blobs as backgrounds, often with grain texture overlay. Vibrant but sophisticated. Feels dynamic and alive.

**Best for:** AI products, creative tools, music platforms, startup launches

**Palette:** Gradients of `#7c3aed`, `#2563eb`, `#06b6d4` on dark `#0f0f0f`

**Prompt snippet:** `Dark landing page with large, flowing gradient mesh backgrounds blending purple, blue, and cyan. Subtle grain texture overlay. White text. Feels dynamic and futuristic.`

---

## 11. Warm SaaS (Lemon Squeezy Style)

**What it looks like:** Light backgrounds with warm accent colors (yellow, orange, coral). Rounded corners everywhere. Friendly and approachable. Playful shadows.

**Best for:** Creator tools, payment platforms, small business SaaS, community platforms

**Palette:** Background `#fffdf7`, accent `#f59e0b`, text `#1c1917`, cards `#ffffff`

**Prompt snippet:** `Warm, friendly SaaS homepage. Cream-white background, amber/yellow accent color, rounded corners on everything. Playful but professional. Inspired by Lemon Squeezy.`

---

## 12. Dashboard Dark (Raycast Style)

**What it looks like:** Matte dark surfaces, subtle card elevation, small rounded corners, icon-heavy navigation. Information-dense but scannable.

**Best for:** Analytics dashboards, admin panels, monitoring tools, CRM interfaces

**Palette:** Background `#0c0c0c`, surface `#1c1c1e`, border `#2c2c2e`, accent `#0a84ff`

**Prompt snippet:** `Dark analytics dashboard with matte black surfaces, subtle card borders, blue accent. Dense data layout with charts, tables, and stat tiles. Inspired by Raycast's dark UI.`

---

## 13. Hand-Drawn / Human Scribble

**What it looks like:** Doodle borders, sketch-style illustrations, handwriting fonts mixed with clean sans-serif. Anti-AI aesthetic. Feels personal and authentic.

**Best for:** Personal brands, indie products, educational sites, children's brands

**Palette:** Background `#fefce8`, text `#1c1917`, scribble `#ef4444`, secondary `#3b82f6`

**Prompt snippet:** `Website with hand-drawn, sketch-style decorative elements. Doodle borders around sections, handwriting-style headings mixed with clean body text. Warm cream background. Authentic and human.`

---

## 14. Bento Grid

**What it looks like:** Asymmetric grid of rounded cards with different sizes. Each card showcases a feature or visual. Apple-inspired modular layout.

**Best for:** Product showcases, feature pages, portfolio grids, about pages

**Palette:** Background `#f5f5f7`, cards `#ffffff`, text `#1d1d1f`, accents vary per card

**Prompt snippet:** `Bento grid layout with asymmetric rounded cards of varying sizes. Each card highlights a different feature with its own visual style. Light gray background. Inspired by Apple's product pages.`

---

## 15. Brutalist Magazine

**What it looks like:** Full-width sections with dramatic type scale, mixed serif/sans-serif, stark color blocks, editorial grid. Loud and opinionated.

**Best for:** Fashion editorials, cultural magazines, art galleries, experimental portfolios

**Palette:** Background alternates `#000000` and `#ffffff`, accent `#ff0000`, type `#000000`

**Prompt snippet:** `Brutalist editorial layout with extreme type scale contrast. Full-width sections alternating black and white. Red accent color. Mixed serif headlines with sans-serif body. Loud, opinionated, magazine-feel.`

---

## 16. Gumroad Creator

**What it looks like:** Simple single-column layout, large product image, bold price, minimal UI. Feels like a creator selling directly. Casual and fast.

**Best for:** Digital products, ebooks, courses, templates, indie tools

**Palette:** Background `#ffffff`, accent `#ff90e8`, text `#000000`, secondary `#23a094`

**Prompt snippet:** `Simple creator product page. Single column, large product mockup, bold price display, pink accent CTA button. Minimal UI, casual feel. Inspired by Gumroad's product pages.`

---

## 17. Fintech Trust

**What it looks like:** Clean lines, subtle card shadows, blue/green trust colors, data visualization elements. Conservative but modern. Security badges visible.

**Best for:** Banking apps, investment platforms, payment processors, insurance

**Palette:** Background `#f8fafc`, text `#0f172a`, primary `#0ea5e9`, success `#22c55e`

**Prompt snippet:** `Clean fintech homepage with trust-building design. Light background, blue primary accent, green for positive numbers. Subtle card shadows, data visualization elements. Security badges in footer. Professional and trustworthy.`

---

## 18. Kawaii / Cute Web

**What it looks like:** Pastel colors, rounded everything, cute illustrations, emoji decorations, bubbly fonts. Hyper-friendly and playful.

**Best for:** Children's products, casual games, sticker shops, fan communities

**Palette:** Background `#fff0f5`, pink `#ff69b4`, blue `#87ceeb`, yellow `#ffd700`

**Prompt snippet:** `Kawaii-styled cute website with pastel colors, extremely rounded corners, bubbly sans-serif fonts, and playful emoji decorations. Pink, sky blue, and gold palette. Friendly and approachable.`

---

## 19. Monochrome Elegant

**What it looks like:** Black and white only (or one accent color max). Strong typography hierarchy does all the work. Minimal decoration. Maximum sophistication.

**Best for:** Architecture firms, photography portfolios, law firms, high-end consulting

**Palette:** Background `#ffffff`, text `#000000`, accent `#000000`, gray `#666666`

**Prompt snippet:** `Monochrome elegant website. Black and white only. Strong serif headings, clean sans-serif body. Typography and whitespace do all the work. No decorative elements. Maximum sophistication.`

---

## 20. Neon Cyberpunk

**What it looks like:** Dark backgrounds with glowing neon accents, scan-line effects, futuristic type. Blade Runner meets web design.

**Best for:** Gaming, crypto/web3, cybersecurity, AI products, tech events

**Palette:** Background `#0a0a0f`, neon green `#00ff41`, neon pink `#ff0080`, blue `#00d4ff`

**Prompt snippet:** `Cyberpunk-themed dark interface with neon green and hot pink glowing accents on near-black background. Scan-line texture overlay. Monospace futuristic typography. Blade Runner energy.`

---

## 21. Nature / Organic

**What it looks like:** Earth tones, organic shapes (blobs, leaves), natural photography, warm textures. Feels grounded and sustainable.

**Best for:** Organic food brands, sustainability companies, outdoor gear, wellness retreats

**Palette:** Background `#f0ebe3`, green `#4a7c59`, brown `#8b6f47`, cream `#f5f0e8`

**Prompt snippet:** `Organic, nature-inspired website with earth tone palette. Sage green and warm brown accents on cream background. Organic blob shapes as section dividers. Natural photography. Sustainable and grounded.`

---

## 22. Swiss/International

**What it looks like:** Grid-based layout, Helvetica-style sans-serif, asymmetric balance, bold use of negative space. Clean, functional, timeless.

**Best for:** Design agencies, architecture, museums, corporate identity, typography-focused brands

**Palette:** Background `#ffffff`, text `#000000`, accent `#ff0000`, gray `#cccccc`

**Prompt snippet:** `Swiss international style website with strict grid layout, Helvetica-style sans-serif typography, asymmetric balance, and bold negative space. Red accent on white. Functional and timeless.`

---

## 23. Dopamine Maximalist

**What it looks like:** Saturated colors everywhere, overlapping elements, mixed fonts, gradients stacked on patterns. More is more. Visual sugar rush.

**Best for:** Fashion for Gen Z, music festivals, streetwear, creative studios

**Palette:** Background `#1a0533`, magenta `#ff006e`, electric blue `#3a86ff`, yellow `#ffbe0b`

**Prompt snippet:** `Maximalist dopamine design with saturated colors, overlapping visual elements, mixed font pairings, and layered gradients. Bold, energetic, visual sugar rush. Think streetwear brand meets art exhibit.`

---

## 24. Soft UI (Neumorphism)

**What it looks like:** Soft shadows (both inset and outer) creating embossed/debossed effect. Monochromatic surfaces. Buttons that look pressable.

**Best for:** Settings screens, smart home interfaces, music players, calculator apps

**Palette:** Background `#e0e5ec`, shadow light `#ffffff`, shadow dark `#a3b1c6`, accent `#6d5dfc`

**Prompt snippet:** `Neumorphic soft UI with dual-shadow embossed effects. Monochromatic light gray surface, buttons that look physically pressable. Purple accent. Clean and tactile.`

---

## 25. Startup Bold

**What it looks like:** Big headline, one strong CTA, gradient hero, simple 3-feature grid. Gets to the point fast. Every YC company ever.

**Best for:** MVP landing pages, product launches, waitlist pages, pitch decks

**Palette:** Background `#ffffff`, text `#111827`, primary `#4f46e5`, gradient to `#7c3aed`

**Prompt snippet:** `Bold startup landing page. Large headline, single CTA button with indigo-to-purple gradient. Social proof logos strip. 3-column feature cards. Simple, direct, conversion-focused.`

---

## 26. Archival Index

**What it looks like:** Grid-based catalog layout with labels, tags, and annotations. Structured data displayed as visual storytelling. Library/museum aesthetic.

**Best for:** Research platforms, digital archives, vintage shops, collection showcases

**Palette:** Background `#f5f2ed`, text `#2c2c2c`, accent `#8b4513`, border `#d4c5b0`

**Prompt snippet:** `Archival index layout with labeled grid cards, tag-based navigation, and annotation-style typography. Warm paper-tone background. Library catalog aesthetic. Structured but visual.`

---

## 27. SaaS Dashboard Light

**What it looks like:** White/light gray surfaces, colored stat tiles, clean line charts, left sidebar nav. The "default good" for business tools.

**Best for:** CRM, analytics, marketing tools, project management, admin panels

**Palette:** Background `#f9fafb`, cards `#ffffff`, primary `#3b82f6`, success `#22c55e`, warning `#f59e0b`

**Prompt snippet:** `Light-themed SaaS dashboard with white cards on light gray background. Blue primary accent, green for success metrics, amber for warnings. Left sidebar navigation. Clean line charts and data tables.`

---

## 28. High-Contrast Accessible

**What it looks like:** WCAG AAA contrast ratios, large click targets, clear focus states, generous font sizes. Designed for everyone, looks clean doing it.

**Best for:** Government sites, healthcare, education, public services, senior-focused products

**Palette:** Background `#ffffff`, text `#000000`, links `#0000ee`, focus ring `#ff6600`

**Prompt snippet:** `High-contrast accessible website meeting WCAG AAA standards. Black text on white, large clickable areas, visible focus states with orange outlines. Generous 18px body text. Clear, usable, inclusive.`

---

## 29. Photography Portfolio

**What it looks like:** Images dominate 80%+ of the viewport. Minimal UI chrome. Hover reveals metadata. Grid or masonry layout.

**Best for:** Photographers, visual artists, fashion lookbooks, travel blogs

**Palette:** Background `#0a0a0a` or `#ffffff`, text `#ffffff` or `#000000`, minimal accent

**Prompt snippet:** `Photography portfolio with full-bleed image grid. Minimal UI, images take up 90% of the viewport. Dark background. Hover reveals title and category. Masonry layout. Let the work speak.`

---

## 30. Notion-Style App

**What it looks like:** Block-based content, slash-command input, sidebar tree navigation, toggle sections. Tool-like interface for end users.

**Best for:** Note-taking apps, wiki tools, knowledge bases, internal tools

**Palette:** Background `#ffffff`, sidebar `#f7f6f3`, text `#37352f`, accent `#2eaadc`

**Prompt snippet:** `Notion-style app interface with block-based content editor, left sidebar tree navigation, and slash-command input bar. Off-white backgrounds, warm dark text. Clean and tool-like.`
