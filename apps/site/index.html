01 About

About the conference

Ten editions in, still run by students.

The tenth edition opens on 20 November 2026 and runs for three days at LRI School in Kalanki. Twelve committees, just over four hundred seats, and a conference put together by the same people who once sat on it.

Nine conferences have run here before this one, each organised by the students of the year that hosted it. This one is no different. The agendas, the country matrices and the running order are all student work, and three faculty advisors from LRI School sign off on the rest.

02 Committees

Twelve committees

Choose your floor.

Every committee is a single delegation: one seat, one delegate, one country. Beyond that each runs its own rules of procedure, its own pace and its own appetite for crisis. Three levels are marked on the cards, and the rooms that want a position paper before you arrive say so.

Seat counts are provisional and may change before allocations go out.
