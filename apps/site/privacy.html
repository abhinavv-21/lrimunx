Delegate privacy

What we hold, and why.

Registering for LRI Model UN X means handing over some information about yourself. This page says exactly what that is, who reads it, and how to get it back or have it deleted.

Last updated 23 August 2026

What the registration form collects

Everything on the form, and nothing that is not: your full name, email address, phone number, school, academic level, your two committee preferences, how many Model UNs you have attended, how many awards you have won, and where you heard about the conference. Two more fields are optional, and are only asked because the answers change how we run the three days: dietary requirements, and anything we need to know to make the venue work for you.

Those two optional fields are the sensitive ones. Leave them blank if you would rather not say. Nothing about your application depends on them.

The payment screenshot

If you upload proof of payment, the image goes to a private storage bucket. It is not on the public internet and it has no shareable address: the only way to open it is a signed link generated for a signed-in member of the secretariat, and that link expires. We keep it because it is the record that your fee arrived.

Two technical details

The server records the IP address and the browser your registration came from. That exists for one reason, which is telling a real application from an automated flood of them. It is not joined to anything else, and nobody reads it in the normal course of running a conference.

What this site does not do

No cookies. No analytics. No advertising, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts of any kind. The typefaces are served from this domain rather than from a font service, so reading a page here tells nobody but us that you did. Nothing is sold, rented or handed to anyone for marketing, ever.

Who can see your registration

The organising committee, through a password-protected operations hub that is not indexed and not linked from this site. Inside it, access goes by role: most of the committee can see delegate names and committee assignments, because that is what running a room requires, while payment records and the budget are limited to the secretariat members who administer them. Every change is written to an audit log with who made it and when.

Where it is kept, and for how long

On servers run for this conference, in a database shared with no other organisation. Registration records are kept while the conference is being organised and for a year after it, because delegates ask for certificates, award confirmations and proof of participation long after the closing ceremony. After that they are deleted.

One deliberate exception: counts with no names in them, such as how many delegates each committee held, are kept as the conference's own record.

If you are under 18

Most delegates are school students, and this conference is run by them. If you are under 18, make sure a parent or guardian knows you are registering and is content with what is on this page. A parent or guardian can write to us on your behalf at any time, and we treat it exactly as we would a request from you.

Asking for your information, or its removal

Write to lrimodelun@gmail.com. We will send you a copy of everything we hold about you, correct anything that is wrong, or delete it, within a week. One thing worth knowing first: deleting your registration before allocations go out means withdrawing from the conference, so we will tell you that and wait for your answer rather than acting straight away.

If this page changes

The date at the top changes with it. If a change affects anyone who has already registered, we email them rather than expecting them to notice.

Questions
